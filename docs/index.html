Notes

A simple, local-first application for notes, follow-ups, routines and habits, pages, kanban organization, and reports. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to sync.

Local-first Offline-ready PWA Optional encrypted sync No tracking

What Notes does

Later

Things to follow up on, with a date to come back to them.

Routines & habits

Counters and timed routines with activity reports.

Pages

Notes and lists that hold more than a task line, with pointers to records.

Kanban

A general board over Later/Page references with have/working/done/deferred columns.

Attachments

Descriptions travel on sync; bytes stay on the device.

Reports

Where your routine time and activity actually went.

Run locally

npm ci
npm run build
npm run dev

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080. Requires Node.js >= 20 and PHP >= 8.0 with pdo_sqlite and mbstring.

Privacy

Your records live in this browser's IndexedDB by default. There is no analytics or tracking. Sync is optional, off by default, and end-to-end encrypted.
